FAQ

Aam sawal
Asking price kya hota hai?

Seller jo price maang raha hai. Bargaining ke baad final price usse kam ho sakta hai.

Kilometres driven kahan se dekhun?

Gaadi ke odometer / instrument cluster par jo reading dikhti hai wahi daalo.

Kya ye mechanic inspection ki jagah le sakta hai?

Nahi. Ye AI-based estimate hai. Photo notes sirf "possible signs" hain. Khareedne se pehle independent mechanic se check zaroor karwao.

Custom model daal sakte hain?

Haan, koi bhi model type karo. Behtar accuracy ke liye uski new/ex-showroom price bhi daalo.
